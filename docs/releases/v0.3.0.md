The second public beta of **Apelles**, and the first one with **Motion**: a free, local video app where an AI agent can cut, animate and grade alongside you.

**Install guide (please read first): https://apelles.studio/install/**

## Download

`Apelles_0.3.0_aarch64.dmg` below. It needs a Mac with **Apple Silicon (M1 or later)** and **macOS 13 or later**.

The app is signed but not yet **notarized** by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS shows *"Apelles" Not Opened*. Click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. You only do this once per download. The install guide has screenshots and a one-line Terminal alternative.

**Updating from v0.2.0 is manual.** Download this DMG and replace the old app — v0.2.0 has no update check in it, so it cannot tell you this exists. Your projects are not touched.

## New since v0.2.0

### Motion is here

The **Motion** tab ships for the first time: a scene-based motion-graphics builder with layers, keyframes with real bezier easing, 2D and 3D cameras, templates and themes, and a render per scene. The render runtime (Node, a headless Chrome and the compositor) downloads in the background the first time you open the tab — editing works immediately, Export waits for it. You can remove it again in **Settings → Motion Runtime**.

### The editor, redesigned

- The **Inspector** is four tabs — Video, Audio, Speed, Info — with values in units you recognise (pixels from the centre, %, degrees, seconds), a value on every collapsed section, and sliders that are one undo step per drag.
- The **Audio** tab is a level in dB with a real meter, pan as L/Center/R, and an EQ you drive by dragging dots on its own graph.
- Redesigned **Export**, **Music edits** and **Clipper (Shorts)** panels, each researched against the tools people actually use and rebuilt from an approved mockup. Music edits can now be **previewed before they are applied**.
- A target-track badge, a zoom slider, a beat-grid popover, marker lines, and the empty states that tell you what to do next.
- **Duplicate a clip** with `Cmd+D`.

### Much faster

- **One GPU render engine** now paints every frame of both the preview and the export, and the preview is handed to the screen without a round trip through the webview. 4K and stacked playback hold 30 fps with the app using roughly half the CPU it did.
- **Decode and encode run in-process on VideoToolbox.** A 60 s 4K export takes about **29 seconds** (0.49x real time) and 1080p about **8 seconds** (0.13x), with the CPU mostly idle.
- Sources that macOS cannot open (`.mkv`, `.webm`, `.avi`, `.mts`, `.mxf`) are converted once on import into optimised media, with progress and a Cancel.
- **Opening a project** is far quicker — a cold open of a large project went from about 12 seconds to under 3 — and shows what it is doing while it works.

### Also

- **Brand kits** — colours, fonts, motion style and references, applied across all three tabs.
- **Beat-synced editing** — on-device beat analysis, a beat grid, snap cuts to beats, and the same beats in Motion.
- **Platform safe-area guides** (Title/Action safe, Shorts, Reels, TikTok) in the preview.
- **Hide from Screen Capture** — the window is excluded from other apps' screen recording, on by default, switchable in Settings.
- **Settings has a gear** in the title bar on every tab. It was previously reachable only by an unlabelled shortcut while one specific tab was frontmost.
- Dozens of editor fixes: an audio-only file can no longer be dropped on a video track, clip lengths can be given in seconds instead of a source's own frame rate, a gap that cannot be closed now says why and points at what blocks it, a swapped clip takes the new file's name, and one unplayable clip no longer stops playback for the whole timeline.

## Known limits

- **macOS on Apple Silicon only**, macOS 13 or later.
- **Not notarized yet** (see above).
- **No automatic updates yet.** This build cannot update itself either; the next release will be able to. Check back here for new versions.
- **A rare stall on very large timelines.** On a timeline of around 300 clips, dragging a clip can very occasionally freeze the window for a couple of minutes before it recovers on its own. We have reproduced it about once in 130 attempts and are still hunting the cause. Nothing is lost when it happens.
- Transcription is tested on English. The on-device model also reads other European languages; Hindi and Hinglish are not supported.
- Motion renders are not yet bit-identical run to run (the individual frames are; the assembled video can differ).
