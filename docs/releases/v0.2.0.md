The first public beta of **Apelles**: a free, open-source, local video app where an AI agent can cut and grade alongside you.

**Install guide (please read first): https://apelles.studio/install/**

## Download

`Apelles_0.2.0_aarch64.dmg` below. It needs a Mac with **Apple Silicon (M1 or later)** and **macOS 13 or later**.

The app is signed but not yet **notarized** by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS shows *"Apelles" Not Opened*. Click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. You only do this once per download. The install guide has screenshots and a one-line Terminal alternative.

## What is in this beta

- **Edit:** a multi-track editor with ripple/roll/slip/slide, transitions, text and captions, per-clip audio, keyframes, and export to video or FCPXML.
- **Transcript editing:** word-level transcripts (Parakeet, on-device) that survive a restart, silence and dark-region detection, and retake review in the **Recut** panel. An agent proposes retake cuts and the app checks every one against the transcript so no unique speech is lost.
- **Colorist:** a GPU grading pipeline with curves, wheels and LUTs, shape masks, AI subject and depth masks, relight, and live scopes.
- **Agents:** any MCP client (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor…) can drive the app. Use the plug icon in the title bar (**Connect an agent**). Edits by an agent can be undone like your own.
- **Everything runs on your Mac.** ffmpeg ships inside the app. AI features set up a local environment (about 2 GB) on first launch, in the background; editing works straight away.

**Not in this beta: Motion.** Its export needs Node.js and a headless Chrome. It returns once the app can download those for you on first use.

## Known limits

- macOS on Apple Silicon only.
- Not notarized yet (see above).
- Transcription is tested on English. The on-device model also reads other European languages; Hindi and Hinglish are not supported.

## Reporting problems

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/theashishmaurya/ApellesStudio/issues) with what you did and what happened, and attach `~/Library/Logs/studio.apelles.app/app.log` if you can.
