FFmpeg and FFprobe bundled with Apelles
=======================================

Apelles.app/Contents/MacOS/ffmpeg and Apelles.app/Contents/MacOS/ffprobe are
unmodified copies of a third-party static build of FFmpeg 9.0.2 for macOS
(Apple Silicon / arm64), made by Martin Riedl:

  Build page:    https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de
  Exact build:   https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/download/macos/arm64/1789931890_9.0.2/
  Build script:  https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script (Apache-2.0)

Apelles runs them as separate programs (child processes). The only change made
to them is the ad-hoc code signature applied to every executable in the app
bundle when Apelles is packaged.

Corresponding source
--------------------
As required by section 6 of the GPL, the complete corresponding source for these
binaries is available:

  - FFmpeg 9.0.2:          https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
  - The build script that produced them, which names and fetches every library
    source above at the versions listed:
                           https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script
  - Each library's own source, from its upstream project (linked from
    https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de).

Apelles' maintainers also offer, for at least three years from the date this
version of Apelles was distributed, to provide a copy of that complete
corresponding source on request, for no more than the cost of physically
performing the distribution. Ask via the Apelles project's issue tracker.
